Sample paper knowledge graph

Built by dataset_creation/build_sample.py from 3 raw paper records — the same parsing and wiring the full-corpus graph uses, minus the MiniLM embedding pass.

Schema

Every raw record becomes one paper node plus a small reasoning chain hanging off it: method → result → evidence → claim → implication. Two of the relations are imposed rather than read from the data: produces and grounds wire a paper's method to its result and its result to every piece of evidence it reports, giving each paper an internal chain instead of a flat star. Only supporting evidence is represented — contradicting_evidence is not read into the graph.

  • in_fieldpaper joins a shared field hub
  • citesintra-corpus citation
  • producesimposed: method yields result
  • groundsimposed: result backs every evidence
  • supportsfrom supporting_evidence
  • impliesfrom claim.implications

Sample paper knowledge graph

Built by dataset_creation/build_sample.py from 3 raw paper records — the same parsing and wiring the full-corpus graph uses, minus the MiniLM embedding pass.

Schema

Every raw record becomes one paper node plus a small reasoning subgraph hanging off it. Two of the relations are imposed rather than read from the data: produces and grounds wire a paper's method to its result and its result to every one of its claims, giving each paper an internal chain instead of a flat star.

Node types

  • paper
  • field
  • claim
  • method
  • result
  • evidence
  • implication

Relations

  • has_claimpaper owns this claim
  • has_methodpaper owns this method
  • has_resultpaper owns this result
  • in_fieldpaper joins a shared field hub
  • citesintra-corpus citation
  • supported_byfrom supporting_evidence
  • challenged_byfrom contradicting_evidence
  • impliesfrom claim.implications
  • producesimposed: method yields result
  • groundsimposed: result backs every claim

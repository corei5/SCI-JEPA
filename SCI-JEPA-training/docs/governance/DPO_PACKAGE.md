# Data-protection package for the TIB data-protection officer

Prepared from the project's artifacts (sci-jepa compliance). Please check, amend and sign.

## 1. Lawful basis (GDPR Art. 6, Art. 89; BDSG § 27 / Lower Saxony data-protection law)
- Purpose: scientific research on machine learning over published research papers.
- Proposed basis: Art. 6(1)(e) (task in the public interest of a public research institution), with Art. 89(1) safeguards; alternatively Art. 6(1)(f).
- Safeguards: data minimisation (no author / affiliation / e-mail fields read), pseudonymous paper ids, no profiling or decisions about people, project storage with group-restricted access, contact data redacted in any release, removal procedure.

## 2. Record of processing (Art. 30), draft entry
- Controller: TIB - Leibniz Information Centre for Science and Technology.
- Data subjects: authors of published papers (indirectly: names cited in summaries, contact data that appear inside paper texts).
- Categories of data: published scholarly texts; incidental names and contact data; no special categories.
- Recipients: project team; published model weights contain no personal data.
- Transfers to third countries: none (all processing on GWDG, Göttingen).
- Retention: copies of the corpus deleted at the end of the project (UrhG § 60d(5)); models and metrics kept.
- Security (Art. 32): GWDG project storage, access restricted to the project group.

## 3. Information duty (Art. 14(5)(b))
- Informing every author is impossible or disproportionate; a project privacy note on the release page describes the processing and the removal contact.

## 4. DPIA screening (Art. 35; EDPB criteria)
- Evaluation / scoring of people: no · automated decisions with legal effect: no · systematic monitoring: no · sensitive data: no · large scale: published texts, incidental personal data only · matching of datasets: no · vulnerable subjects: no · innovative technology: yes (ML) · preventing exercise of rights: no.
- Result: one criterion of nine; a full DPIA is not required (to be confirmed).

Every job that reported in the last 15 minutes is shown, on any node or cluster; older ones are listed under "ended jobs". The hosted site shows a job once its cluster has pushed (about every 30–60 s). On MIG slices the driver reports memory per slice but no utilization per slice; power and temperature are those of the whole physical GPU, which other jobs' slices share. "Max. time left" and "Finishes" assume the run trains to its maximum epoch count at its recent speed (early stopping usually ends it sooner) and leave out the final test evaluation, which takes about an hour at full scale. Loss: the JEPA loss averaged over the last few seconds and over the current epoch. Validation: the latest validation pass, at an epoch end or a live pass for the curves. Runs resumed from a checkpoint continue their step count.
